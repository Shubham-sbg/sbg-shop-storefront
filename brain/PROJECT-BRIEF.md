# SBG SHOP — Project Brief

## Product

SBG-branded direct-to-consumer e-commerce store; mobile-first website + protected Admin Panel + PWA; initial market India. Product families: SBG jerseys/apparel, other merchandise and digital products.

## Product/design objective

Create an original, premium virtual jersey showroom inspired by the supplied video. The visual anchor is a quiet cool-grey/charcoal studio with a horizontal hanging garment lineup, subtle camera/product-focus transitions and minimal labels. Adapt the environment to SBG's established black, deep-purple, gold and white identity. Keep product art accurate and the store usable, accessible and fast.

## Supplied black jersey family

- Black body with subtle dark texture.
- Purple shoulder/side panels and gold trims/cuffs/collar accents.
- SBG eagle crest on the front.
- Rear design includes SHADOW / 07; one provided variant also includes `# EVERY MOVE MATTERS`.

## Supplied white jersey family

- White patterned body.
- Purple shoulders/side panels, gold accents, black/gold collar/cuff details.
- SBG eagle crest on front.
- Rear design includes SHADOW / 07; a provided variant includes `#every move matters`.

Preserve the logo, spelling, geometry, colour and typography in source artwork. Do not infer that every image is a separate SKU. Exact products/variants require owner confirmation.

## Reference-video principles

- Garments are shown as a horizontal collection in a studio-like virtual showroom.
- Wider display and closer product-focus views alternate.
- Product selection is visual and accompanied by a small label.
- Motion is restrained and supports product discovery.
- The reference store's name, exact copy, code and protected assets must not be reused.

## Payments

Build main storefront/admin workflows first. A real payment gateway will be added later when the owner provides/selects it. No live payment now. If checkout test states are needed, mark them TEST ONLY; never fake live success. Keep production launch blocked until real payment integration is verified.

## Primary environment

Google AI Studio Build mode is preferred for eventual development, subject to current compatibility validation. The `brain/` protocol is tool-independent and must be followed if the owner also uses Cursor, OpenCode or Antigravity.
